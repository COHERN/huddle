Walk Details

How this walk is scored

30 questions a day: 5 Daily Essentials (always shown) plus 10 High, 8 Medium, and 7 Low Risk, pulled at random from the full question banks — point values follow Ecolab's risk-based model. Every walk starts at 100; each failed item deducts points by risk level — Essentials −7, High −7, Medium −4, Low −1. A score of 90 or above is a PASS, anything below is a FAIL. All 5 Daily Essentials and 2 of the High Risk items are Auto-Fail — failing any one of those drops the score straight to 0 regardless of everything else, since those are the true non-negotiables (soap and hand sinks, proper hot/cold holding, sanitizer strength, no expired product). Ratings: 97–100 Best in Class, 93–96 Very Good, 80–92 Good, 70–79 Needs Improvement, 60–69 Underperforming, under 60 Poor. Mark N/A and a different question from the same risk level swaps in — you'll never get stuck with one that doesn't apply.
